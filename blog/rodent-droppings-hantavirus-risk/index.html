Rodent Droppings Cleanup and Hantavirus Risk

2026-08-15

Rodent infestations leave more than a mess. Droppings and urine can carry pathogens, including hantavirus in some regions, along with salmonella and other bacteria. Disturbing dried droppings without proper protection can aerosolize particles and increase exposure risk.

Where Problems Appear

Attics, basements, crawlspaces, and vacant units are common sites. Older housing stock in Newark—and storage areas in multi-family buildings—can harbor long-term infestations that go unnoticed until a unit turns over or renovations begin.

Safe Remediation Approach

Professional rodent droppings cleanup uses PPE, wetting methods that reduce dust, careful removal, disinfection of affected surfaces, and disposal practices appropriate to the contamination level. Pest control should address the active infestation so droppings do not return.

When to Call

Large accumulations, unknown duration of infestation, or any respiratory concern related to rodent exposure warrant professional response rather than a broom-and-mask approach. Landlords preparing units for new tenants should resolve droppings contamination before turnover.
