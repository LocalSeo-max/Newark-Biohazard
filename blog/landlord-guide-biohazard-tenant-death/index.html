Landlord Guide: Biohazard Cleanup After a Tenant Death

2026-08-01

A tenant death in a rental unit can leave biological contamination that maintenance staff are not trained or equipped to handle. Landlords who send untrained employees into those conditions create liability and health risk. Professional biohazard remediation is the appropriate response.

First Actions

Allow authorities to complete their work. Do not authorize cleaning until the scene is released. Notify your insurer early. Document the unit condition through the provider assessment rather than through untrained staff photos alone.

Who Pays

Coverage depends on the landlord policy and the circumstances of the death. Many commercial and landlord forms respond to sudden events; gradual or excluded conditions may not. Confirm with the carrier. Security deposits and estate claims are separate issues that do not replace professional remediation standards.

Multi-Family Buildings

In Newark apartment stock, contain the unit, protect hallways, and coordinate with management on HVAC and adjacent units. Incomplete cleanup can lead to complaints, code issues, and re-leasing delays.
